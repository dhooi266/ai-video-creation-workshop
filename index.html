Programme overview

Most companies still outsource every piece of video they publish — and wait weeks, and pay per revision. This workshop moves that capability inside your team. In two days your people learn to produce professional visual and video content with AI: advertisement videos, product and service promos, cinematic storytelling, music-video styles and AI avatar presenters.

Participants learn structured prompting and repeatable production workflows that apply directly to marketing, training, branding, social media and corporate communication. Every module is demonstrated, then practised, then reviewed — so the team leaves with work they actually made, not notes about it.

Where it helps, we tailor the practice briefs to your industry and brand, so the videos produced on day two are assets you can genuinely use.

Why companies run this

  • Cut turnaround on campaign and product video from weeks to days
  • Reduce spend on outsourced production and stock footage
  • Publish more consistently across social and internal channels
  • Build training and onboarding videos without a studio
  • Give the team a practical, confident first win with AI
